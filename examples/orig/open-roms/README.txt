open_roms_kernal.orig and open_roms_basic.orig (in examples/orig) are the
Kernal and BASIC ROM images of MEGA65's Open ROMs, a free replacement for the
Commodore 64's ROMs, used as tests of commodore.c64() (see
python/open_roms_kernal.py and python/open_roms_basic.py).

They are bin/kernal_generic.rom and bin/basic_generic.rom, unchanged, from

    https://github.com/MEGA65/open-roms

at commit ad178dbe4d48cd6a317737a8e0e7e662f7e33d32 (2026-05-16),
where their source code is. They are distributed under the licences in
LICENSE (in this folder): the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or
later (COPYING.LESSER, which builds on the GNU General Public License in
COPYING), and for some BASIC files the MIT licence.
