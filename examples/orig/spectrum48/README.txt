spectrum48.orig (in examples/orig) is the ZX Spectrum 48K ROM, used as a test
of the Z80 on a real program (see python/spectrum48.py).

The ROM is copyright (C) 1982 Sinclair Research Ltd, now owned by Amstrad plc.
Amstrad have kindly given their permission for the redistribution of their
copyrighted material but retain that copyright. (Cliff Lawson, Amstrad plc,
in comp.sys.sinclair and comp.sys.amstrad.8bit: images of the ROMs may
be included with emulators, unmodified, as long as the copyright messages
aren't altered, and nobody may charge for them.)

It's the image in Debian's spectrum-roms package (version 20081224-5,
usr/share/spectrum-roms/48.rom), unchanged.

The names of the routines and tables in python/spectrum48.py are those of
The Complete Spectrum ROM Disassembly by Ian Logan and Frank O'Hara
(Melbourne House, 1983), as used in Geoff Wearmouth's assembly listing of the
ROM (also in Debian's package, usr/share/doc/spectrum-roms/zx82.htm), from
which the addresses were taken.
